13F Holdings Dashboard

Source: SEC EDGAR Form 13F-HR ·
Values reported on Form 13F-HR (long positions only; excludes shorts, cash, fixed-income, private holdings). Total values are computed by summing the value field of all rows in the information table. Re-run python3 scripts/fetch_13f.py after editing the FILERS list to refresh data.

Add a 13F filer

Look up filings on EDGAR. Submitting adds an entry to scripts/fetch_13f.py's FILERS list (you re-run it to pull data). Until then, the tab is staged but data won't load.
